# Marrow

A mobile app for saving videos in a form you can follow. Share a TikTok, Instagram or YouTube Shorts link and get back a card, such as a recipe with a grocery list, a workout or a travel plan, linked to the original video.

Coming soon on iOS.

## How it works

- It reads what's said in the video and what's shown on screen, and turns both into a structured card.
- Each item on the card is checked against the video. Anything the video doesn't mention is left blank and marked as missing.

## Built with

React Native, Expo, TypeScript, Supabase, FastAPI, Gemini, SQLite, Jest
